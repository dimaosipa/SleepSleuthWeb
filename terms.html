2. Description of the App

SleepSleuth is a macOS menu bar utility that monitors power assertions — system-level flags that prevent your Mac from sleeping. The App displays which processes hold these assertions, provides tools to investigate or terminate user-owned processes, can pin selected apps or command-line tools to keep your Mac awake, and can optionally summarize local coding-agent token usage from sources you authorize.
